# Managed Agent onboarding example

Claw Me collects the use case, explains the paid add-on and temporary inference allowance, and opens Stripe Checkout. No runtime is provisioned until the verified webhook confirms payment.

**Claw Me:** Where do you want to meet your Agent first?

- Hosted OpenClaw gateway
- Messaging app
- Email

After provisioning, Claw Me opens the selected surface. The managed Agent resumes the existing session instead of repeating answers and guides the owner through a secure durable-provider handoff.

The Claw Me-funded allowance ends when $5 is consumed or after 30 days and never auto-charges.
