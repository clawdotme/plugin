# Security

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made against the latest published plugin and contract release. Upgrade to the latest release before reporting an issue that may already be fixed.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately to `security@claw.me`. Do not open a public issue containing credentials, personal data, payment details, private Page content, or an exploitable proof of concept.

Please include:

- The affected plugin, skill, API, or contract version.
- The security impact and the prerequisites for exploitation.
- Minimal reproduction steps using accounts and data you control.
- Relevant request IDs or redacted logs, without secrets or personal data.
- A safe way to contact you.

We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days, complete initial triage within five business days, and provide a progress update at least weekly until resolution. These are response targets rather than a service-level agreement.

## Researcher safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy. Use only accounts and data you own or are authorized to test; avoid privacy violations, service degradation, social engineering, persistence, data destruction, and accessing more data than needed to demonstrate impact. Stop testing and report immediately if you encounter sensitive data. Do not demand payment as a condition of disclosure.

Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before public disclosure. This safe harbor does not authorize violating third-party systems or laws.

## Repository scope

This public repository contains instructions, schemas, prompts, and client configuration. Hosted authorization, payment, provisioning, fraud, secret handling, and enforcement controls remain server-side. A contract that promises a control is not evidence that the hosted service can be bypassed; reports should demonstrate an actual security impact.
