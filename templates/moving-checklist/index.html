Pack for the first evening

Keep essential clothing, chargers, basic kitchen items, and important documents accessible. Decide who carries the essentials bag so it is not accidentally loaded with the last boxes.

Coordinate the dependencies

A confirmed key handover comes before booking a tightly timed delivery. Record the owner and contact route for each arrangement in the private plan.
