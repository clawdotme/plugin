Partnership Proposal · Example edition

A small collaboration worth testing.

Common Room Studio and the fictional Side Project Circle are considering a joint workshop. The proposal starts with one session and a shared review rather than a long-term commitment.

The working agreement

ContributionStudioCommunity
PreparationCreate the workshop materialGather participant questions
DeliveryFacilitate one practical exerciseHost and welcome participants
Follow-upWrite a short recapCollect voluntary feedback
ReviewAssess effort and learningAssess member usefulness

Why try it

The studio can learn where independent builders get stuck, while the community receives a practical session on clearer project briefs. Neither side needs to promise leads, revenue, or a permanent relationship.

Pilot boundaries

Run one ninety-minute workshop for a small group. Agree how names, recordings, and participant material may be used before the event. Each partner retains ownership of its existing material.

Continue only with evidence

After the session, compare preparation time, participation, and feedback. Decide together whether to repeat, change, or stop. Any sponsorship or payment arrangement belongs in a separately reviewed agreement.
