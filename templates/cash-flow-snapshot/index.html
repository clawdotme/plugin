How to read it

Each closing amount equals opening cash plus receipts minus payments. The next month starts with the previous closing amount. This example ends December at €4,500.

The timing question

October has the largest net outflow in the sample. Check when receipts are expected and which payments are committed before using the scenario to make a decision.

Keep assumptions separate

Replace expected amounts with verified records as they become available. A cash-flow view is not the same as profit, and it should not silently mix tax, credit, or personal funds.
