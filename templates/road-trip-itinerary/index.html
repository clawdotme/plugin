Build around the driver

Set a driving plan that suits the actual drivers and conditions. Do not treat an estimated arrival time as a reason to skip breaks. Share responsibility for navigation and practical arrangements.

A useful stop list

Choose a primary rest stop and an alternative for each longer leg after checking that they are open and suitable. Keep booking and contact details in the private itinerary.

If the weather changes

Move the optional activity or shorten the day. The plan should make it easy to stop early and communicate a change, with accommodation and route alternatives verified in advance.
