This fictional journal demonstrates how to record an idea without copying a source at length. The sample reading is an invented essay called “The Useful Handover,” not a published reference.

Summarize in your own words

Write the argument as you understand it and distinguish the author’s position from your response. Keep any quotation short, accurately attributed, and linked to its real source in the actual journal.

Test the connection

The useful connection is not simply that two subjects sound alike. Describe what would change in your work if the idea were true, and what observation might challenge it.

Return to the entry

At the next review, record whether you used the idea, changed your mind, or need better evidence. A journal becomes useful through retrieval, not through the number of entries.
