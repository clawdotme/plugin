Fieldnote is a fictional project notebook for small teams. This example demonstrates the product story and a preview of its core workflow; product capabilities and availability are illustrative.

See the first project

A café website starts with the five pages it needs. The weekly note identifies the missing menu copy. The decision log records why online ordering is outside the first release. Each piece links back to the same brief.

Who it helps

Teams with a few concurrent projects and a named owner for each one. A readable project record can help collaborators join the conversation without searching through a long message history.
