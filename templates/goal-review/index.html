Goal Review · Example edition

Decide what deserves another month.

This fictional monthly review covers three goals for an independent builder. The record separates effort from outcome and makes room to stop work that no longer fits.

GoalEvidence this monthDecision
Finish handover exampleReviewed with two readersContinue
Write four articlesOne draft completedReduce to one useful article
Try a new planning appNo clear problem identifiedStop

What moved

The handover example produced direct reader feedback, which is useful evidence of progress. The article goal exceeded the available writing time. The app experiment lacked a concrete need.

What to change

Choose one article based on the questions readers actually asked. Keep a small weekly writing slot and define a finished draft before adding a publication target.

What to release

Stopping the app experiment frees attention for work with a clearer purpose. Record why it stopped so the same idea does not return as an unexplained obligation next month.
