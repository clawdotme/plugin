Experience with context

Robin’s sample work covers onboarding, customer communication, and internal knowledge tools. This profile deliberately describes the work performed rather than inventing client logos, credentials, or performance claims.

A useful first week

Agree the question and the available evidence. Speak with the people closest to the work, review one real workflow, and separate immediate changes from assumptions that need a test.

Check the fit

This service suits a team that can name a decision owner and share relevant material. A request to replace an entire product strategy without access to customers needs a narrower first step.
