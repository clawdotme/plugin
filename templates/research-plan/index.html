Research Plan · Example edition

A study small enough to answer one question.

This fictional study asks whether a document index helps a new project owner find the approved material. It is a planning example with a small exploratory sample.

The research question

Can a person unfamiliar with the project find the approved menu and identify its update owner? Keep the task stable enough that observations can be compared.

Method and care

Explain the purpose, obtain consent, and let participants stop. Observe behavior without coaching toward the answer. Store any recordings or identifying notes only where the participant agreement permits.

Decision use

The study can identify usability problems and suggest revisions. Six exploratory sessions do not establish a population-wide effect. Decide in advance what evidence would justify another iteration.
