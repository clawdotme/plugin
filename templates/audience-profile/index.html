Understand the person making the decision.

This fictional profile describes an independent café owner preparing a first website. It is a working hypothesis for research, not a demographic fact about all business owners.

The working agreement

DimensionWorking hypothesisWhat to verify
TaskMake hours and menu easy to findRecent visitor questions

ConcernWho maintains the site after launch?Past supplier experiences

The situation

The owner is balancing the website project with daily operations. A useful service explains the required inputs and review moments in practical terms rather than expecting the owner to learn design terminology.

Likely questions

What do I need to supply? How many reviews are included? What happens when the menu changes? Treat these as questions to test in conversations, not quotations from a real customer.

Evidence to collect

Speak with relevant owners about a recent project decision. Record differences and exceptions, then update the profile. Avoid adding age, income, or personality traits that do not explain the task.
