Referral Program Brief · Example edition

Make introductions easy to understand.

This fictional studio is considering a simple referral pilot. The program is not active; reward terms and local requirements need review before any offer is made.

The pilot purpose

Encourage relevant introductions from people who understand the studio’s work. A useful referral is a good fit for both sides, not simply a name added to a list.

Terms to decide

Define who can participate, which projects qualify, when a reward is earned, and what happens if work is cancelled. Keep the wording plain and have the actual terms reviewed before launch.

Measure the quality

Track relevant introductions, qualified conversations, and participant questions. Review whether the process creates confusion or pressure before expanding it.
