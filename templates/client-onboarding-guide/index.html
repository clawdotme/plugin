Common Room’s fictional café project begins with one decision maker and a shared brief. This guide explains what the client supplies, what the studio prepares, and when the first review happens.

Working agreement

Use one review thread for each deliverable and identify the person who consolidates feedback. Urgent issues go to the project lead; a new idea goes into the scope discussion before work begins.

Access without confusion

List the systems required and the minimum role needed in each. The client grants access through the system’s own invitation process. Keep passwords and recovery codes out of this guide.

Ready for the first review

The kickoff is complete when the goal, the five-page scope, the content owner, and the first review date are understood by both sides. Missing inputs become named actions rather than hidden assumptions.
