Order decision

Notebooks are below the chosen reorder point. Confirm next month’s attendance and the supplier’s pack size before placing an order. Pens, badges, and paper remain above their sample thresholds.

Count with context

Record damaged or reserved stock separately from usable stock. If two counts disagree, reconcile the shelf location and unit before adjusting the total.

Keep the record current

Name the person who completed the count and the time of the snapshot. A purchase or event after that time changes available stock and should trigger an update.
