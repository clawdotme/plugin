Typography in use

Use Archivo for operational labels and dense information. Use Bitter for document headings. Keep headings descriptive and avoid squeezing text with excessive letter spacing.

Color with a purpose

The green accent identifies a useful next action or active state. Status labels need words as well as color. White and quiet neutral surfaces keep long documents readable.

Write like a colleague

Prefer “Review the project brief” to “Unlock your project potential.” State what a control does and describe an outcome that the artifact can actually support. Keep implementation notes in source documentation.
