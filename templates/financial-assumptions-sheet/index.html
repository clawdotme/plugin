Base scenario

Two projects at €2,400 produce €4,800 in modeled revenue. Subtracting €1,200 in direct project costs and €1,800 in fixed costs leaves €1,800 before items not included in this simplified model.

One-project scenario

At one project, modeled revenue is €2,400 and the same calculation leaves €0. This illustrates sensitivity to project volume; it is not a complete profit or cash-flow calculation.

What remains outside

Tax, timing, owner compensation, financing, and other relevant items are not modeled here. Add the actual inputs and have the model reviewed before relying on it for a material decision.
