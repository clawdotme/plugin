Practice the conversation you are likely to have.

This fictional beginner Dutch practice focuses on ordering a drink in a café. It is a compact learning example with ordinary phrases and a suggested role-play.

Try the exchange

Read the exercise and worked guidance

One person greets the customer and asks for the order. The learner orders a coffee, says thank you, and asks to pay. Repeat slowly before trying a natural pace.
