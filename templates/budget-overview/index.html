02 / Reading the numbers

Simple formulas.
Visible assumptions.

Remaining = planned − actual.
Example: €2,500 − €2,100 = €400.

Used = actual ÷ planned × 100.
Example: €2,100 ÷ €2,500 × 100 = 84%.

Not included yet

  • Tax and currency conversion.
  • Committed but unpaid costs.
  • Bank balances or cash-flow forecasts.
  • Invoices or connected accounting data.

Unknown figures should be marked unknown, never silently entered as zero.
