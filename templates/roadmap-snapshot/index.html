Why this order

Ownership addresses the immediate handover problem. Reminder behavior depends on a meaningful review date. Bulk import is useful only if larger libraries are a verified need.

What can change

Research, delivery effort, and operational constraints may change the sequence. Record the reason for a change rather than silently moving an item between horizons.

Review the roadmap

At the next planning review, compare each item with evidence and capacity. Keep committed work distinct from exploratory ideas so the audience understands the confidence behind the plan.
