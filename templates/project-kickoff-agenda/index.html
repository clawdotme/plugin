Project Kickoff Agenda · Example edition

Start with the decisions that unlock the work.

This fictional sixty-minute kickoff starts a five-page café website. The purpose is to confirm the goal, scope, responsibilities, and first review rather than solve every design question in one meeting.

00–10

Business goal and visitor needs

Output: Shared problem statement

10–25

Pages and exclusions

Output: Agreed first-release scope

25–40

Content and responsibilities

Output: Named input owners

40–50

Schedule and review process

Output: First review date

50–60

Read back decisions

Output: Checked action list

Before the meeting

Share the draft brief and ask participants to bring current content and known constraints. Invite the decision maker, content owner, and delivery lead; a large audience is not required.

During the discussion

Keep unresolved questions visible. If a topic cannot be decided with the available evidence, give it an owner and a next step instead of forcing agreement.

Close with a read-back

Read the scope, exclusions, owners, and first review date aloud. Ask participants to correct the record, then prepare a follow-up draft for review before sending.
