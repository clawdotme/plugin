Come with a question

Bring a project you can describe in two minutes, or a question you are actively working on. Finished work is welcome, but an honest account of an unfinished experiment is equally useful.

A comfortable afternoon

Allow space between sessions and provide seating throughout. Publish verified step-free access, restroom details, transport options, and a contact for access requests when the venue is confirmed.
