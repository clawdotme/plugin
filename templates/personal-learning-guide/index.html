Study plan Notes
A small, steady practice

Make room
for something new.

A four-week guide to learning the basics of photography. Less collecting links, more making time to practice.

The goal

Tell a story
in five photos.

The rhythm
3 × 30

Three short sessions each week

What you need

A camera.
Your curiosity.

A phone is a good place to start.

One step at a time

Week 1 · Notice Look for light, lines, and everyday details.
Week 2 · Compose Try three ways of framing the same subject.
Week 3 · Edit Select your strongest images and explain why.
Week 4 · Share Arrange five photos into a short visual story.

After each session

  • What did I try?
  • What surprised me?
  • What will I do differently next time?
