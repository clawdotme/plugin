Service Comparison · Example edition

Choose the support the project actually needs.

This fictional studio offers three engagement shapes. The comparison helps a client choose between a focused review, a guided sprint, and a complete website project. Prices are intentionally omitted until scope is known.

OptionIncludesBest fit
ReviewOne existing flow and written findingsA team ready to make its own changes
SprintDiscovery and one tested directionAn unclear problem with a deadline
WebsiteFive-page design, build, and handoverA business launching its first site
Compare the responsibility

In a review, the client implements the changes. A sprint produces a direction and supporting evidence. The website engagement includes implementation within a defined page scope.

Questions before choosing

Who can supply content? Who will approve the work? Is the deadline fixed? The answers can change the suitable engagement more than the number of features listed.

A sensible recommendation

For the café example, the website engagement fits because the owner needs a complete handover and has no internal build team. Revisit the recommendation if the client already has a reliable implementation partner.
