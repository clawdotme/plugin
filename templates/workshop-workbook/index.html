Exercise: name the task

Read the exercise and worked guidance

Start with “A visitor needs to…” and finish with something observable. Worked answer: “A visitor needs to find today’s opening hours on a phone.” This is easier to test than “make the site engaging.”

Exercise: set a boundary

Read the exercise and worked guidance

List the smallest useful result and one tempting addition to exclude. Worked answer: publish the menu and location; keep online ordering outside the first release.

Exercise: choose the check

Read the exercise and worked guidance

Describe how another person will verify the result. Worked answer: ask a reader to find the hours and menu, record where they hesitate, and revise the unclear path.
