# Template artwork

Eight distinct hero photographs generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-07. Original generated PNGs are bundled without remote image dependencies. Existing Lisbon, portfolio-studio and learning-workspace photos are retained for those individual templates.

Shared prompt: Landscape 3:2, 1536 × 1024. Premium editorial photography, realistic materials, soft natural lighting, cream with restrained sage/coral accents, centered subject for cropping. No people, logos, watermarks, readable text, borders, UI or collage.

## client-project-proposal.png

An overhead editorial photograph of a client project planning table: cream architectural paper, neatly arranged muted green material swatches, a brass ruler and a closed coral folder on a pale oak conference table. No open book, no plant by a window.

## weekly-team-brief.png

An airy modern team room with a large pinboard displaying an orderly arrangement of blank cream, sage and coral cards, a round meeting table and three empty chairs. Wide architectural interior photograph.

## project-progress-report.png

Close editorial photograph of a scale architectural model under construction: pale wooden blocks forming a small modern building, a few measured components and a metal caliper on a blue-grey workbench. Precision, progress and thoughtful work.

## product-launch-page.png

A premium product launch still life: a sculptural coral-orange portable speaker with no branding on a sage plinth, soft peach curved backdrop and a small directional pool of warm light. Minimal contemporary product photograph.

## presentation-outline.png

A calm empty contemporary presentation space with a white projection screen, one coral lectern and rows of light wooden chairs seen diagonally from the back. Soft daylight, muted sage accents, no text on screen.

## product-interface-prototype.png

## meeting-follow-up.png

Editorial photograph of an empty small meeting table immediately after a productive conversation: three different ceramic cups, closed notebooks in sage and ochre, a capped pen and stacked index cards. Warm daylight, no readable writing, no open book.

## research-decision-brief.png
