Team Offsite Plan · Example edition

Leave with decisions, not just notes.

This fictional one-day offsite gives a small team time to review its working habits and choose two improvements. The venue, catering, and access arrangements remain items for the organizer to confirm.

Prepare lightly

Ask each person for one example of work that flowed well and one that became difficult. Share the agenda early and allow written contributions for people who think better before a discussion.

Facilitate the decision

Describe the problem before proposing solutions. Give quieter participants time to contribute, record disagreement accurately, and make the decision rule clear.

Follow through

The offsite is complete when the experiments have owners, a small first step, and a review date. Keep the recap short enough that the team can return to it during ordinary work.
