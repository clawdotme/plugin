Define the boundary

The landscape concerns project knowledge for small teams, not every collaboration product. State what is inside and outside the analysis before comparing approaches.

Look for unmet work

A team may already store files successfully but struggle to transfer responsibility. Investigate that specific task rather than assuming the market needs another all-purpose workspace.

Evidence needed

For a real assessment, collect current product observations, customer behavior, and credible market sources. Separate known facts, estimates, and hypotheses in the final map.
