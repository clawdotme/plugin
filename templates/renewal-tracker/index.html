Start with the agreement

Record the actual notice period, renewal term, and cancellation route from the current contract. A reminder date should leave time to decide and complete the required process.

Review the usefulness

Ask whether the service is used, whether the plan fits, and whether any data needs to be exported before a change. Keep the current price and renewal quote separate.

Record the decision

Each owner marks renew, change, or end with a reason and evidence of the completed action. A planned cancellation is not a completed cancellation.
