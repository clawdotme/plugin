Before doors open

Walk the room, check the display from the back row, and confirm who can make a timing decision. Keep the contact sheet private and available to the delivery team.

If a session runs late

Protect the closing time and shorten discussion before removing the break or access buffer. The host communicates the change clearly rather than silently pushing every later cue.

If equipment fails

Continue with the printed exercise and verbal instructions. A useful workshop should still work when the display is unavailable. Record any incident that affects the next event.
