Current position

The first website is complete in this example. The immediate opportunity is a clear maintenance routine, not a speculative rebuild. Record the client’s stated priorities separately from the studio’s ideas.

Relationship risks

Updates may stop if responsibility is unclear. A seasonal menu change could expose an outdated page. Keep the next review date and the owner of each content area visible.

Ninety-day focus

Check the information once a month, review actual visitor questions, and discuss improvements only when there is evidence of a need. A real plan should include verified commercial history in the private version.
