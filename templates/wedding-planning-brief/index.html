Three priorities

Time with guests, comfortable access, and a meal that can be enjoyed without rushing. Use these priorities to judge optional additions rather than expanding the plan by default.

Guest experience

Confirm dietary and access needs through an appropriate private channel. Provide verified travel information and a clear schedule once the venue is booked.

Open decisions

Agree the budget range, booking deadlines, and who can approve a supplier. Keep personal guest information out of any public version of this page.
