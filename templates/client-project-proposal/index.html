What Success Looks Like

Customers can find a location, check opening hours, and browse the menu in a few clear steps.

Delivery checkpoints

Week 1 · Agree the content

Review the current site and choose the five pages. The café supplies opening hours, menu, location details, and approved photography.

Weeks 2–3 · Design and build

Review the page designs, then build responsive layouts. Check the menu and location on a phone before the second review.

Week 4 · Review and handover

Check content, accessibility, and links. Supply the launch checklist and a short guide for routine updates.

Scope assumptions and exclusions

Includes five informational pages and two review rounds. Booking systems, online ordering, copywriting beyond supplied content, and ongoing maintenance are separate work.
