01 / Scope

Useful from the first handover.

  • A proposal page with scope, milestones, and budget.
  • A static weekly update layout for progress and decisions.
  • Responsive Claw Me styling and clearly marked sample content.
  • Two review rounds and a guide for updates with your Agent.

Outside this scope

Live integrations, databases, authentication implementation, payments, and ongoing operations. These pages present information; they do not run those services.

02 / Delivery

Three weeks. Clear checkpoints.

Week 1 · Discover

Agree the audience, source content, success criteria, and review owner.

Week 2 · Build

Prepare the layouts and review the first version on desktop and mobile.

Week 3 · Handover

Apply feedback, check content, and hand over the update guide.
