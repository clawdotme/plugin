Project Brief · Example edition

Agree what this project needs to do.

The fictional Greenhouse café needs a website that answers common visitor questions on mobile. The first release covers location, opening hours, the menu, the story, and contact information.

The working agreement

QuestionWorking answerHow to check
Who is it for?A first-time visitor on a phoneReview three visitor tasks
What matters most?Hours, location, and menuFind each in one clear path
What is excluded?Ordering and reservationsKeep outside launch scope
Who decides?The café ownerOne consolidated review

The problem to solve

Customers currently ask for information that exists in several places. The site should make the essentials easy to find and simple for the owner to keep current.

Acceptance conditions

Use approved content, readable type, working links, and layouts that fit mobile screens. The owner should be able to explain the update process at handover. Record any unresolved item before launch.

Open questions

Confirm seasonal hours, the menu source of truth, and the person responsible for updates. Do not fill missing business details with plausible-looking information; mark them for the owner to supply.
