This fictional statement of work covers the design and handover of a five-page café website. It is a planning example for review, with commercial and legal terms to be supplied by the contracting parties.

Responsibilities

The studio prepares the design and build. The client provides approved text, licensed images, and one consolidated response per review round. Delays to those inputs require the schedule to be revisited together.

Change handling

Write down the requested change, its reason, and its effect on time and cost. Work outside the agreed scope starts only after both parties accept the revised plan.

Before agreement

Replace all sample details, attach the agreed pricing and payment schedule, and have the parties review the terms appropriate to their jurisdiction. This example is not an executed contract.
