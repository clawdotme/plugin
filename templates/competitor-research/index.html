Start with the user’s choice

A competitor is not always another branded tool. A familiar folder or a manual routine may be the real alternative, with lower setup effort and known limitations.

Record observations precisely

For a real product comparison, note the version, plan, date, and tested workflow. Keep marketing claims separate from behavior you actually observed.

Draw a bounded conclusion

The index offers explicit context with a modest setup in this example, but it creates a maintenance task. Test whether that tradeoff helps the actual receiving team before recommending a switch.
