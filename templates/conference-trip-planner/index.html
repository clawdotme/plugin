Choose fewer sessions

Select the sessions that support a real learning question. Leave time to compare notes and meet people instead of treating every timetable gap as an obligation.

Keep logistics together

Record booking references only in the private version. Include venue access information, local time, transport alternatives, and a contact for travel changes after verifying each detail.

After the trip

Write the useful findings while they are fresh. Separate ideas worth testing from contacts to follow up with, and review any message before sending it.
