Choose by the work

Start with the outcome you need, your deadline, and the material already available. A provider’s specific scope is more useful than a broad label such as full service.

Before an introduction

Ask about availability, relevant examples, who will do the work, and how costs are agreed. Confirm the provider’s current details independently before making a purchase.

Keeping the directory useful

Assign an owner to each listing and record its last verification date. Remove unavailable providers and identify paid placements clearly if the real directory includes them.
