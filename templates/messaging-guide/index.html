Say the same useful thing, clearly.

This fictional studio’s message is about clear scope and useful handover. The guide helps collaborators describe the service consistently without claiming outcomes that have not been measured.

Lead with the task

For an owner preparing a first website, explain what they receive and how the project proceeds. “A five-page website with an update guide” says more than “a transformative digital experience.”

Adapt without drifting

A short headline can emphasize the handover; a proposal can explain responsibilities and acceptance. Both should describe the same actual service.

Claims to avoid

Do not invent customer counts, awards, conversion improvements, or guaranteed results. When real evidence becomes available, attach its source and the conditions under which it was observed.
