Decision Log · Example edition

Keep the reason beside the choice.

This fictional project log records three choices for a café website. It preserves the reasoning available at the time, including conditions that would justify reopening a decision.

IDDecisionOwnerRevisit when
D-01Launch without online orderingCafé ownerOrdering becomes a funded priority
D-02Keep menu as readable page textStudio leadMenu maintenance becomes impractical
D-03Use one feedback ownerProject leadReview ownership changes
D-01: a smaller first release

The team chose a five-page site to answer visitor questions first. An ordering integration would add operational work that the café is not prepared to own in this example.

D-02: content people can read

Readable page text keeps menu information available without requiring a download. The owner must still maintain the source whenever dishes or prices change.

D-03: coherent feedback

One person consolidates comments so the studio receives a clear response. This does not prevent others from contributing; it identifies who resolves conflicting requests.
