Three priorities

Finish the brief, review a working example, and hand over the agreed result. New work should be compared with those commitments before it is added to the week.

A daily reset

At the end of each day, record what actually moved and the next useful step. Carry unfinished work forward deliberately rather than copying an entire unchecked list.

Friday review

What was completed, what changed, and what should stop? Use the answers to plan the next week. The aim is a credible schedule, not a perfect record of busyness.
